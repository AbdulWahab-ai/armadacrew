# Refund policy

Refunds are allowed for duplicate charges, unused prepaid credits, and platform outages that blocked the paid feature.

## Thresholds

- Under $500 USD: support may refund with a ticket.
- **Over $500 USD: human approval is mandatory** (HITL gate).
- Chargebacks: do not refund; escalate to risk.

## Duplicate charges

If CRM shows `duplicate_charge_usd` matching the customer complaint, treat as confirmed. Reverse the later capture, keep the original invoice.

## SLA

Billing tickets: first response 4 business hours. Enterprise (Ada Lovelace, Hopper Labs) same-day.
